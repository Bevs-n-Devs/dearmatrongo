Why We Collect Your Information & How You Can Request Deletion
Our Policy We collect your personal details to ensure that, if you choose to pursue a claim, third parties may contact you for further assistance. Your information is securely stored and will never be shared without your consent. If you wish to delete your data from our records, we require certain details to accurately locate and remove your report. You will need to provide your name, email, date of the incident, and the name of the facility the report was made against. Additionally, we will ask for your email address (or an alternative email of your choice) to send a confirmation once your data has been deleted.
Storing Your Data Your privacy and security are our top priorities. All personal information you provide is securely encrypted before being stored in our database. This means that even we cannot directly access your data in its original form. Encryption ensures that your sensitive details remain protected and can only be decrypted when strictly necessary, such as for verification purposes. We do not share your personal data with third parties unless you choose to make a claim, in which case, only the relevant details will be securely provided to the appropriate parties.
Finding Your Data To ensure your data privacy, we securely encrypt all personal details when storing them. When you request data deletion, we encrypt the information you provide and compare it to our database to locate your record. If a match is found, we decrypt it for verification, delete the entry, and send you a confirmation email. If we cannot find a match, we will notify you and provide guidance on how to refine your request for further review.
